# Triple Triad

Deployed on Netlify: [triple-triad.netlify.app](https://triple-triad.netlify.app/)

## Game rules

Triple Triad is played on a 3-by-3 board. You (blue) face the enemy AI (red),
and each side starts with a hand of five Marvel cards. You always play first,
then turns alternate: pick a card from your hand and place it on any empty
cell — even one with no adjacent card.

Every card carries four numbers arranged in a cross in its top-left corner.
Each number, from 1 to 10, is the strength of one side of the card: top,
right, bottom and left.

When you place a card next to an enemy card, the touching sides are compared.
If your side shows the higher number, the enemy card flips to your color — it
is captured. A well-placed card can capture several neighbours at once: played
in the center, it challenges up to four cards at the same time.

Only the card being placed can capture. Cards already on the board never flip
a newly placed card, even if their touching side is higher — so placing a weak
side against a strong enemy card is safe. A captured card is not lost either:
it can change colors again later in the game.

Every card you own is worth one point: cards of your color on the board, plus
the cards still in your hand. Both players therefore start at 5–5, and the
scores shift with every capture.

The game ends when the ninth card fills the board. The player with the higher
score wins — the last unplayed card still counts as a point for its owner.
Equal scores mean a draw.
